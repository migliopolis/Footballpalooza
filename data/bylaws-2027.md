# What changed for 2027

These are the v5.0 by-laws rewritten for 2027. Every change is tagged [[CHANGED]], [[NEW]] or [[REMOVED]]; untagged text is unchanged. Dates for the rookie draft, keeper deadline and auction are TBD.

| Area | Change for 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| Lineup / roster | SuperFlex becomes the 9th starter; bench 11; 20 active + 3 IR + 5 taxi |
| QB keepers | Max 2 veteran QBs plus any rookie QBs from that year's rookie draft |
| Rookie salaries | Published formula (NFL draft capital × FBP pick) for rounds 1–2; maxes RB $30, WR $15, QB $12, TE $8; round 2 floor $3; rounds 3/4 = $2/$1 |
| Taxi squad | 5th spot; taxi rookies count at two-thirds of salary; deferred third, trade fee; TS assignments final 24 hours before the auction |
| Playoffs | Full tiebreaker ladder incl. all-play wins; 3rd-place game in Week 17; division winners always outrank wild cards |
| Rookie draft order | Picks 1–12 fully specified (3rd-place game decides picks 9–10) |
| Divisions | No realignment for 2027 |
| Trades | No roster parking: traded players move to the new team when the trade is processed; no holding a player for another team |

# Article I – League Commissioner

Paul Miglio

# Article II – League Entry Fee

$150 per team, due before the NFL season begins, paid by PayPal, Apple Pay, Zelle, etc. to the commissioner.

# Article III – Prize Payout Structure

Total pot $1,800 ($150 × 12 teams), less the MFL fee before payouts. Champion 50%, 2nd place 30%, 3rd place 15%, regular-season high score 5%. Percentages apply to the post-fee amount. 3rd place is the winner of the Week 17 3rd-place game.

# Article IV – League Setup


- 12 teams in 3 divisions (East, Central, West), based on geography.
- Divisions are reviewed every 3 years (Article XIII). The 2027 review found no owner relocations, so **divisions stay as they are for 2027**.
- 14-week regular season; each team plays division opponents twice and all others once.
- 3-week playoff tournament (Weeks 15–17).
- No set end date; owners will be notified if one is set.

# Article VI – Player Salaries & Contracts

## Rookie Salary Scale

[[CHANGED]] The 2024 positional table is **removed**. Rookie salary is now set by a formula combining the player's NFL draft capital with his FBP rookie-draft pick, for **Rounds 1–2**, capped by a position maximum:

| Position | Maximum (pick-1 salary) |
| --- | --- |
| RB | $30 |
| WR | $15 |
| QB | $12 |
| TE | $8 |

- [[CHANGED]] Round 2 picks are no longer a flat $3: salaries come from the same formula and bottom out at **$3**.
- Round 3 picks = **$2**. Round 4 picks = **$1**.

### [[NEW]] Rookie Salary Formula (Rounds 1–2)

Salary = **position max × NFL factor × FBP factor**, rounded to the nearest dollar (half up), with a **$3 minimum**.

- **Position max:** the pick-1 salary in the table above.
- **NFL factor:** set by the player's overall NFL draft pick. Find the pick in the anchor table below; between two anchors the factor slides in a straight line; after the last anchor it stays flat. Undrafted players count as pick 260.
- **FBP factor:** set by the player's rookie-draft slot, 1.01 through 2.12 (table below).

*Example:* a RB taken 10th overall in the NFL (factor 0.95) and 1.03 in the FBP rDraft (factor 0.85) = 30 × 0.95 × 0.85 = 24.2, which rounds to **$24**.

## Rookie Contracts

- Duration: 5 years, plus the 6th-year option (see Taxi Squad below).
- Escalators: +$3 / +$6 / +$9 / +$12 annually.
- Cuttable with no penalty.
- Promotion during the rookie year starts escalators early.

## Veteran Contracts

- Duration: 3 years; escalators +$3 (Year 2), +$6 (Year 3); cuttable with no penalty.
- Waiver pickups are signed to 3-year vet contracts.

## Taxi Squad & 6th-Year Eligibility

- Only rookies from the current rDraft are TS-eligible.
- A rookie who stays on TS all rookie season earns the 6th-year option.
- Promotion during the rookie year starts the salary escalators and voids the 6th year.
- **Frozen year-1 salary:** a rookie who spends his full rookie season on TS keeps his draft salary into Year 2 (no escalator). Escalation begins in Year 3, which yields a 6-year contract.
- [[CHANGED]] TS now has **5 spots** (Article V).

## [[NEW]] Taxi Squad Salary Discount

1. **Two-thirds rule.** For salary-cap purposes at the auction, a TS rookie counts at **two-thirds of his salary, rounded up**. The remaining third is *deferred*.
2. **TS designation deadline.** Because the discount changes auction budgets, TS assignments must be final **24 hours before the auction starts**. The commissioner then adjusts each team's auction budget. A rookie not designated by the deadline is active and cannot be moved to TS later (Article IX).
3. **Who takes the discount.** The team holding the rookie on TS at the designation deadline. A trade before the deadline passes the discount to the buyer.
4. **If he stays on TS all season,** the deferred third is forgiven.
5. **If he is promoted during his rookie season** (even if later traded), the deferred third becomes **dead cap against the discount-taker's budget the next season**. Teams may agree side terms (e.g., "if promoted post-trade, Team A owes Team B $x").
6. **Trade fee.** If a team trades away a TS rookie after the deadline, it owes a fixed fee equal to **the lesser half of the deferred amount (rounded down)**, charged once to the discount-taker next season, regardless of what the buyer does.
7. **Buyer.** A team that acquires a TS rookie after the deadline received no discount. If it promotes him it owes **no dead cap**; it only starts escalators early and loses the 6th-year option.
8. **No discount** on salaries of $1–$2.

| Rookie salary | Counts at (2/3) | Deferred (1/3) | Trade fee | Fee leakage\* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $30 | $20 | $10 | $5 | $5 |
| $15 | $10 | $5 | $2 | $3 |
| $12 | $8 | $4 | $2 | $2 |
| $10 | $7 | $3 | $1 | $2 |
| $8 | $6 | $2 | $1 | $1 |
| $5 | $4 | $1 | $0 | $1 |
| $3 | $2 | $1 | $0 | $1 |

\*Leakage = deferred amount not recovered if the player is traded without promotion.

## Trade Scenarios

- TS players traded between TS teams retain 6th-year eligibility if left untouched.
- Once promoted, they lose TS and 6th-year eligibility.
- See the Taxi Squad Salary Discount rules for the cap treatment of traded TS rookies.

# Article VIII – Keeper Selection

- [[CHANGED]] Deadline: set by the commissioner each year (TBD). *Was "typically one week before the Veteran Auction."*
- Cap: total keepers + $1 per open roster spot must stay under the $200 salary cap. [[CHANGED]] Open spots are measured against the 20-man active roster. TS rookies count toward the cap at their Article VI cost, not toward roster limits.
- [[NEW]] **QB limit:** a team may keep at most **2 veteran QBs**, plus any rookie QBs drafted in that year's rDraft. A QB who spent his rookie year on TS may be kept into Year 2, but he uses one of the 2 veteran QB spots.
- Keeping zero players is allowed.

**Jordy Nelson Rule.** If a keeper suffers a **season-ending injury** and is placed on IR by his team after the deadline but before the auction, one such player may be dropped to regain his salary.

# Article IX – Veteran Auction

- Platform: FBP League Site.
- [[CHANGED]] Timing: TBD each year. *Was "last Sunday of August."*
- Eligibility: undrafted rookies, free agents, and players not kept.
- Contracts: all auction-acquired players sign 3-year contracts.
- Nomination order: prior season's standings; the champion nominates first.
- [[NEW]] **Taxi designations lock 24 hours before the auction starts.** Rookies are designated to TS (or left active) by that deadline; the commissioner then adjusts each team's auction budget for the TS discount (Article VI). A rookie left on the active roster at the deadline cannot be moved to TS afterward. *This replaces the old practice of carrying a rookie on the active roster through the auction and moving him to TS before kickoff.*

# Article X – Waivers

- Processing Wed–Sun weekly at **12 PM EST**, Week 1 through Week 17.
- 24-hour lock on dropped players.
- **Saquon Barkley Rule:** players with season-ending injuries go on a manually curated "Can't Add List"; claims on them are voided and refunded.

# Article XI – Trades

- A trade is live once accepted, and must be processed through the league site.
- Conditional picks are allowed if terms are declared.
- Bullying or harassing any owner over a trade is not allowed.
- Tradable assets: players (with contracts), draft picks (up to 2 years out), auction cash, FAAB dollars.
- [[NEW]] Trades of TS rookies carry the discount and fee rules in Article VI. Teams may attach side terms to a TS trade, declared in the trade.
- [[NEW]] **No roster parking.** A player may only be rostered by the team that owns him, and no team may hold or store a player for another team for any length of time. Every player in a trade moves to the receiving team's roster when the trade is processed. An agreement to trade for a player but leave him on the other team's roster, to be moved over at a later date, is not allowed, and neither is any other arrangement to hold a player now and hand him to another team later. Any such deal is treated as collusion (Article XVI).

# Article XII – Playoffs

## Qualification

Six teams advance:

- **Seeds 1–3:** the 3 division winners. The two with the best records are seeds 1 and 2 and receive a first-round bye; the third is seed 3.
- **Seeds 4–5:** the two best remaining records, regardless of division.
- **Seed 6:** the remaining team with the most all-play wins (record against every team each week).
- [[NEW]] A division winner always outranks a wild card in seeding, regardless of record.

## Playoff Matchups

- **Week 15:** #4 vs #5; #3 vs #6.
- **Week 16 (semifinals, reseeded):** #1 vs the lowest remaining seed; #2 vs the highest remaining seed.
- **Week 17:** Championship (semifinal winners). [[NEW]] 3rd-place game between the two semifinal losers; the winner is 3rd place, the loser 4th.

## [[CHANGED]] Tiebreakers

**Division title.** Teams in a division play each other twice.

- Two teams tied: head-to-head record. A 2–0 sweep wins. If split, total regular-season points.
- Three teams tied: combined head-to-head record among the three. If a team drops out, restart the process among those remaining. Still tied: total points.
- Still tied: all-play wins, then coin flip.

**Full ladder by purpose** (all-play wins count everywhere; a coin flip is always the last resort):

| Purpose | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Division title | Head-to-head | Total points | All-play wins |
| Seeding division winners (1–3) | Record | Total points | All-play wins |
| Wildcards (seeds 4–5) | Record | Total points | All-play wins |
| Seed 6 | Most all-play wins | Total points | Record |
| Rookie picks 1–6 | Lowest Potential Points | Total points (lowest picks first) | Fewest all-play wins |

*Was: head-to-head, total points, all-play win percentage, coin flip, applied to everything.* All-play wins replace all-play win percentage.

# Article XV – Salary Cap

$200 cap per team. FAAB is $100 per team, separate from the cap; cap funds do not roll over into FAAB.

# Article XVI – Tanking & Lineup Compliance

All owners field a full, legal starting lineup each week. Non-compliance: 1st, friendly reminder; 2nd, commissioner warning; 3rd, $10 FAAB fine; 4th, $10 auction-cash fine (next season); further instances may cost draft position or trigger commissioner review. Context and intent are considered; the goal is accountability.

**Collusion** of any kind (trade manipulation, deliberate losing) brings immediate commissioner intervention and possible removal.
