Four trades in seven days, and three of them sent a playoff contender's 2027 1st out the door. We gave each deal to one of our columnists for a grade on both sides. Then we handed all four to Devin on the analytics desk, who doesn't care about anybody's feelings.

One thing to keep in mind as you read: no pick is locked in yet. Every slot below is a projection from the standings through week 7, and they'll keep moving until the season's over. The same goes for contracts. A salary on the books for 2027 or 2028 only matters if somebody decides the player is worth keeping at that price.

## Fully Cooked lands James Cook

@by peg

- **Fully Cooked get:** RB James Cook
- **Wholey Club get:** Fully Cooked's 2027 1st (projected 1.09) and $10 of 2027 auction cash

Everybody wants to talk about Cook's 113.6 points in six starts. I want to talk about his contract. He's in year five of a six-year rookie deal at $38. Next year the escalator takes him to $50, at 28, in the last year of the deal. Is a $50 James Cook a keeper? Maybe, if he finishes the way he started. It's close enough that Fully Cooked should treat him as a rental with an option, not a cornerstone.

Is a rental worth a 1st and $10 for a 5-2 team that's third in points? Probably. Cook's been a top back all year, and that pick projects late. But $10 is 5% of an auction budget, and Fully Cooked will feel it in August whether they keep Cook or not.

Wholey Club are 3-4 and dead last in points, so selling makes sense even if it looks like waving the white flag with the playoffs still in reach. They turned a back who's about to get expensive into another 1st and extra auction cash. Wholey's own pick projects to 1.02, Fully Cooked's to 1.09, and they'll have $210 to spend next summer.

**Grades: Fully Cooked B · Wholey Club B+**

## Thots go all in on McCaffrey

@by rex

- **Thots & Players get:** RB Christian McCaffrey
- **Sour Grapes get:** Thots' 2027 2nd (projected 2.08) and $10 of 2027 auction cash

Flags fly forever. Write it down.

Thots lead the league with 920 points and they're 4-3, which tells you this league isn't always fair. So they went and got the back with 144 points in seven starts. Yes, he's 30. Yes, his hamstring is barking and San Francisco is on bye this week. He's expected back for week 9, and that's all I need to hear in October.

Let's be honest about what this is: a rental. He's $54 next year. If everything breaks right this season, maybe somebody keeps a 31-year-old back at that price. Nobody's keeping him at $60 the year after. So Thots paid a late 2nd and $10 for seven weeks and a playoff run, and for a rental of the league's best-producing back, that's a fair price.

Sour Grapes are 2-5 with a 0.2% chance at the playoffs, and McCaffrey wasn't going to be their keeper either. Turning a player they'd likely have let go into a pick and $10 is exactly what a seller should do.

**Grades: Thots & Players B+ · Sour Grapes B**

## Son of Tinactin cash in on Breece Hall

@by dana

- **Mailing It In get:** RB Breece Hall
- **Son of Tinactin get:** RB RJ Harvey, Mailing's 2027 1st (projected 1.12) and Mailing's 2027 3rd (projected 3.12)

This is how you rebuild. SOT's own pick projects to 1.01 right now, and they just added a second 1st and a 3rd. They also got RJ Harvey: a 25-year-old back at $8, rising to $14 and $23, and very keepable at those prices. Harvey had 76 points this year, mostly from Mailing's bench, and scored 14 in his first start for SOT.

Mailing It In are 5-1-1, the defending champions and the 1 seed, so I understand the impulse. Hall can win a playoff week by himself. But he's out this week with a quad injury, he has 42.5 points this season, and next year he costs $44. On this year's numbers, that's not a keeper unless he comes back strong. So Mailing may have rented a hurt back for two months, and paid for it with a 1st, a 3rd and the cheap young back they could have kept for years.

Mailing are buying a December, and SOT just bought three years.

**Grades: Son of Tinactin A · Mailing It In C+**

## Bullish buy Ja'Marr Chase

@by sal

- **Bullish on da Bears get:** WR Ja'Marr Chase, their own 2027 3rd back (projected 3.11) and MR. FROBOT's 2027 4th (projected 4.07)
- **Jesus Christ College get:** Bullish's 2027 1st (projected 1.11), Bullish's 2028 2nd and $5 of 2027 auction cash

Sixteen years on this beat, and I've learned one thing about first-place teams that rank eighth in scoring: they either go get somebody or they go home. Bullish went and got somebody. Ja'Marr Chase is 26 and he's put up 97 points in six starts for a team that was losing anyway.

Now, the kid next to me made me look at the contract, and he's right. Chase is $62 this year, $65 next year and $71 after that. Nobody's keeping him at $71, and $65 is a stretch. So this is a rental, and Bullish paid a 1st, a future 2nd and five bucks for seven weeks and a playoff run, while getting two picks back. For a team that wants a ring, I'll take that.

Here's where I change my mind on JCC. I wanted to yell at them for selling a young WR1. But they weren't keeping him at those prices either. A 1-6 team turning a player they'd have let go into a 1st, a 2nd and $5 isn't a fire sale. It's housekeeping.

**Grades: Bullish on da Bears B+ · Jesus Christ College B**

## The analytics desk: who can keep what they bought?

@by devin

Grades are nice. Here's what each side actually took on. I scored every trade two ways.

**This season:** the starter points each buyer expects to add over the last seven weeks of the regular season. Each projection blends a player's 2025 and 2026 per-game scoring and skips games he's already listed out for.

**Next two seasons:** what each side has that's worth keeping in 2027 and 2028.

- **Players:** a player only counts in a future year if his projected points beat what his salary would buy at the auction. This year, a dollar spent on a starter at the auction has bought about 4.5 points. If he isn't worth keeping, he counts for nothing that year, because his owner just lets him go. Running backs lose 10% a year from 28 and 20% from 30.
- **Picks:** worth what that slot has actually produced in this league, minus the rookie salary. They're projected slots, so they'll move. FBP rookies drafted from 2021 to 2024 scored this many points in their first two seasons:

| Slot | Avg. points, first 2 seasons |
| --- | --- |
| 1.09–1.12 | 148 |
| Late 2nd | 91 |
| Any 2nd | 101 |
| Late 3rd | 62 |
| Mid 4th | 19 |

- **Auction cash:** counted at the same 4.5 points a dollar, so $10 is worth 45 points.

Here's the keeper check for every player who moved:

| Player | 2027 salary | Keep? | 2028 salary | Keep? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ja'Marr Chase | $65 | No | $71 | No |
| Christian McCaffrey | $54 | Coin flip | $60 | No |
| James Cook | $50 | Coin flip | — | Deal ends |
| Breece Hall | $44 | Probably not | $50 | No |
| RJ Harvey | $14 | Yes | $23 | Yes |

Four of the five are rentals or coin flips, and the one sure keeper in the bunch went to a rebuilding team.

| Trade | Buyer, this season | Buyer, next 2 seasons | Seller, next 2 seasons | Future paid per point now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| McCaffrey | Thots +129 | −45 | +109 | 1.2 |
| Chase | Bullish +112 | +32 | +208 | 1.6 |
| Cook | Fully Cooked +125 | −45 | +157 | 1.6 |
| Hall | Mailing +78 | 0 | +312 | 4.0 |

The last column is the price tag. It's how many points of future value each buyer gave up for every point it adds this season. Lower is a better deal.

**The sellers win all four on future value.** That's how a rental works: you trade next year for this year. The real question is who paid a fair price.

**Thots got the best rental.** McCaffrey adds the most points this season, about 21 a week, and Thots paid the least for him. **Bullish and Fully Cooked paid about the same price**, around 16 to 18 points a week for 1.6 points of future value each. Bullish at least got two picks back.

**Mailing paid more than three times what Thots did.** Hall is hurt and probably not a keeper, and the deal also cost them Harvey, the only real keeper who moved all week. For the defending champs that buys a better playoff ceiling, and little else.

And Sal: welcome to the spreadsheet.

**Devin's winners:** this season, Thots, Bullish and Fully Cooked. Next two seasons, Son of Tinactin by a mile, then Jesus Christ College, Wholey Club and Sour Grapes. Biggest overpay: Mailing.
