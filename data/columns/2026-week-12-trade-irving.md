Two trades in three days, and a week before the Dec 3 deadline. This one went through early Thanksgiving morning.

- **MR. FROBOT get:** RB Bucky Irving (TB), $14, year 3 of 3
- **Sour Grapes get:** $6 of 2027 auction cash

## MR. FROBOT: a rental with a purpose

Irving has 178.1 points, 17.8 a game in the 10 games he's played, with 21 touches a game. Three times he's been a top-3 running back for the week. His Trade Value is 5,339.

FROBOT didn't have a hole at running back. Javonte Williams (191.4) and Kyren Williams (164.3) are already one of the league's best pairs. Irving's job is the flex. FROBOT's next-best backs, Cam Skattebo and Alvin Kamara, have averaged under 10 points a game. Irving is roughly a touchdown a week better.

The timing makes sense. FROBOT are 7-4 and lead the Central, with a 52 percent chance at a bye and 28 percent at the 1 seed. Their last three games are Mailing It In this week, then Hauk Tua and Hold Timers, and they're favored in all three, between 61 and 65 percent. In a race for a week off, a touchdown a week matters. Kyren Williams already put up 16.5 Wednesday night, so FROBOT lead Mailing before Irving plays a snap for them.

The catch: Irving is in the last year of his deal. He goes back to the auction next summer, and FROBOT can't keep him. So they're paying $6 of next year's budget for Irving the rest of this season: three regular-season weeks and the playoffs. Their 2027 budget drops to $194.

## Sour Grapes: paid for a player they were losing anyway

Sour Grapes are 3-8 and out of the race. Irving was headed to the auction either way, so selling him now turns a few meaningless weeks into $6 they can spend in the auction. With the $10 they got from Thots & Players in the McCaffrey deal, they're now at $216 for 2027, the most in the league.

There's a draft angle too, the same one Lainey pointed out with the Pickens trade. Sour Grapes pick 5th right now with 1,352.9 potential points, 22 behind The Fist of the Magi at No. 4. Take a 17.8-a-game back out of their best possible lineup, and that number grows more slowly for the last three weeks. Passing Magi for the fourth pick is very much in play.

## Grades

**MR. FROBOT: A-.** A cheap price for a real upgrade, at the one spot they could make one, in the middle of a bye race. They gave up no players and no picks. The only cost is $6 next summer.

**Sour Grapes: B+.** They got something for nothing and may move up a draft slot doing it. $6 won't change their auction, but it beats watching Irving walk for free.
