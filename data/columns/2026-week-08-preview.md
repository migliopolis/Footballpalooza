Sixteen years I've been covering this league, and the first thing they teach you is this: look at the standings. The second thing, they teach you now, is don't trust them. Half the league is separated by one game, and the other half is already staring at the draft board. Six teams sit at 4-3 or better, and only two of them get byes.

Defending champion Mailing It In (5-1-1) holds the 1 seed on a five-game win streak. Week 8 hands them the one team nobody wants to see right now. And Fully Cooked goes into East leader Bullish's building with a bye on the line. Let's go to the board.

## The matchups

The win chances come from the site's computer, which plays out the rest of the season 20,000 times. I didn't trust it either. It's been annoyingly right. Series records count every meeting since 2019, playoffs included.

### Fully Cooked (5-2) at Bullish on da Bears (5-2) — game of the week

The computer makes Fully Cooked a 72% favorite, and I can't argue. They've won three straight by an average of 36. Bullish leads the East at 5-2, but their 706.5 points rank eighth of 12, and their all-play record is 30-47. In my day we called that "winning ugly." Now they call it "unsustainable," and I hate that they're probably right.

Bullish does own this series, six wins in eight meetings. A Fully Cooked win pushes their bye chances to 47%. A Bullish win keeps their bye hopes alive at 30%, and they're 86% to win the East either way.

### Mailing It In (5-1-1) at Hold Timers (4-3)

The closest game on the board: Mailing a 56% favorite. Hold Timers are the hottest team in the league, averaging 132 over the last three weeks and winning their last two by 76 and 55. Mailing has won eight of ten meetings, and champions know how to play in somebody else's barn. But this one moves Hold Timers' playoff odds 28 points — 76% with a win, 48% with a loss. Nobody on the slate has more riding on a single Sunday.

### Thots & Players (4-3) at Jesus Christ College (1-6)

Thots lead the league in points (920.0) and the power rankings, and they're 4-3. Explain that one to me. They're an 80% favorite against a JCC team that has lost six straight and averaged 94 over its last three. Thots lead the series 7-5. Win and they're 95.5% to make the playoffs; lose and they drop to 84.5%.

### The Fist of the Magi (4-3) at Son of Tinactin (2-4-1)

Fist is a 78% favorite, but they've dropped two straight, including a 74-point clunker in week 7. They lead the Central only on the total-points tiebreak, so this one matters: a win lifts their playoff odds to 77.5%, a loss sinks them to 55%. SOT leads the series 4-3 and needs this just to stay in double digits (11.6% with a win, 2.8% with a loss).

### MR. FROBOT (3-4) at Wholey Club (3-4)

FROBOT is the fourth-best scoring team in the league sitting at 3-4, holding the 6 seed on all-play wins, and an 80% favorite. Wholey has the league's lowest point total (632.6) and won last week by two-tenths of a point. I've seen a lot of football. I have not seen much of that. FROBOT leads the series 5-3; win and it's 77% playoff odds, lose and it's 57%.

### Sour Grapes (2-5) at Hauk Tua (3-4)

Hauk Tua is a 66% favorite sitting at 17% playoff odds, so call it a must-win: 21% with a win, 7.5% with a loss. Sour Grapes has scored under 100 in two of its last three and is at 0.2% to make it. The series is tied 4-4, which is the most interesting thing I can tell you about this one.

Now, the bottom. Every year somebody asks me who's tanking. Every year I tell them the same thing: you can't, not here. Non-playoff teams pick in order of **lowest potential points** — the best lineup you could have started every week. Losing doesn't move you up. A bad roster does. Son of Tinactin holds the 1.01 today.

What makes this year fun is the extra picks floating around:

- **Son of Tinactin** owns Mailing It In's 1st from the Breece Hall trade, projected 1.12. SOT could open round one and close it.
- **Wholey Club** owns Fully Cooked's 1st from the James Cook deal, projected 1.09. Wholey ought to be pulling for Bullish this week, and for an early Fully Cooked playoff exit.
- **Jesus Christ College** owns Bullish's 1st from the Ja'Marr Chase trade, projected 1.11. JCC wants the opposite.

The prize at the top is Ohio State receiver Jeremiah Smith, the clear consensus 1.01 in the early superflex rookie rankings. After him it bunches up: Texas WR Cam Coleman, Florida RB Jadan Baugh, Texas QB Arch Manning and Indiana WR Charlie Becker all average between picks 4 and 6. Picks 1.02 through 1.05 are where the real decisions happen, and Wholey, Sour Grapes and JCC are separated by one good week apiece.

## Before I go

- **Upset watch:** Hold Timers over Mailing It In (44%). Nobody's scoring more right now.
- **Points vs. record:** Thots (920.0) and FROBOT (887.7) are first and fourth in scoring at a combined 7-7. Bullish is 5-2 on the eighth-most points. Something gives in the second half. It always does.
- **Biggest swing:** Hold Timers' playoff odds move 28 points on one game. Fist's move 22.
- **Lowest bar:** Wholey and JCC combined for 140.2 points last week. Wholey won. I watched the whole thing so you didn't have to.
