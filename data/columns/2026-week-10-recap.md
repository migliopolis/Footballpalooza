Two games mattered this week, and both of them delivered. One was the best game on the card. The other ended with a 0.9-point margin, a sweep, and the best press conference this league has seen in years. I've been doing this sixteen years. I took notes.

## Hold Timers win the Detroit civil war

### Hold Timers 148.8, Hauk Tua 131.7

I said this was the game of the week, and it was. It was the highest-scoring game of Week 10 at 280.5 combined, and Hold Timers' 148.8 was the top score in the league.

Detroit split it. Jahmyr Gibbs put up 44.2 for Hauk Tua, the second-best game of the week by anyone. Amon-Ra St. Brown played through the rib injury and gave Hold Timers 25.8. Gibbs won that duel by 18.4. Hold Timers won the game by 17.1, because Hold Timers had help. Chase Brown 28.3, Trevor Lawrence 26.8 with Bo Nix on bye, and Blake Corum 20.9 filling in for D'Andre Swift. They lost three starters to byes and still put up the best score in the league.

Hauk Tua will look at the bench and wince. Drake Maye gave them 3.0. Kirk Cousins scored 15.4 sitting next to Quinshon Judkins' 16.1. Hauk Tua's best possible lineup would have scored 153.5. That wins this game.

This was the game that decided which of these two holds a playoff spot going into Week 11, and the computer reacted about the way I said it would. Hold Timers went from 49% to 70% to make the playoffs. Hauk Tua went from 67% to 48%. Hold Timers are back in the 5 seed at 6-4. Hauk Tua are 5-5 and the first team out. Hauk Tua still lead the series, 7-5, but this was the one that counted. FROBOT probably take the Central. These two are probably fighting over one spot, and right now Hold Timers have it.

## Thots sweep, and Collin takes the podium

### Thots & Players 137.4, Fully Cooked 136.5

Christian McCaffrey scored 46.3. That's the top score in the league this season, by anyone, starting or sitting. It made him Player of the Week, and it was the difference in a game decided by 0.9 points. Josh Allen added 22.3 and the Vikings defense 23.0. Thots also set the perfect lineup: 137.4 scored, 137.4 possible.

Fully Cooked did plenty. Patrick Mahomes went for 35.4, Nico Collins 24.4, James Cook 21.7. But they left Samaje Perine's 13.9 on the bench and started Malik Nabers, who gave them 7.4. Their best possible lineup was 143.0. Without Puka Nacua, who's on IR, there wasn't much room for error.

If this felt familiar, it should. In Week 4 Fully Cooked scored 153.7, still their best week of the season, and lost to Thots 157.7 to 153.7. Tetairoa McMillan put up 45.2 in that one, which was the comeback. Here's the number that matters for what came next. There have been exactly two 45-point games in this league this season. McMillan's 45.2 and McCaffrey's 46.3. Both came for Thots. Both came against Fully Cooked.

That's a sweep for Thots, their fifth straight win in the series, and a 10-4 lead in it since 2019. Fully Cooked fell from the 2 seed to the 4 and lost first place in the West. Their odds of a bye went from 61% to 40%.

Then came the handshake line. Paul M. says Collin D. said something there I won't print. Then Collin got to the podium.

"Needing miracles is never a good thing," he said about Thots' big games against him. He called McMillan and McCaffrey "a bum and a white guy." And he went on: "Having guys go nuclear twice in a season... something that could possibly never ever happen again in their entire careers against me and have that be why he won is legit rare air of luck." And he finished with this: "It's insane. He's needed 2 all-time career games in both our matchups. It's as simple as that."

I checked his numbers, because that's the job. He's half right. McMillan's 45.2 is the best game of his FBP career by more than 12 points. McCaffrey's 46.3 isn't even his best. He put up 48.7 in Week 4 of 2023.

On going nuclear, the site's Heat Check counts every starter who hits the nuclear line for his position. Fully Cooked's starters have gone nuclear eight times this season, tied for third-most in the league. Kenneth Walker III has done it three times by himself, Nacua twice. Thots' starters have done it five times. And Thots' opponents have gone nuclear against them 10 times, tied for the most in the league. One of them was Mahomes on Sunday. Big games happen to everybody. Fully Cooked just ran into two in the same building.

Paul M. had his own podium. "He thinks it's lucky Christian McCaffrey has the high score for the week," he said. "While he's over there getting 35 from Patrick Mahomes on a $2." He's right about that one. Mahomes cost $2 at the auction and he's second for Best Value. Paul M. kept going: "Talk about luck, he stumbled (expletive) first into a 4th round Puka Nacua." That one checks out too. Nacua was pick 4.04 in 2023. Paul M. closed with: "I'm riding high right now. I just swept a division rival and I'm in his head rent free."

Thots are the 6 seed. Fully Cooked are the 4. A playoff meeting isn't out of the question. I'd like a seat for that one.

## The rest of the card

### MR. FROBOT 136.7, The Fist of the Magi 80.9

Joe Burrow 33.2, Parker Washington 28.7, CeeDee Lamb 20.2. FROBOT also left Cam Skattebo's 27.2 on the bench and won by 55.8 anyway. That's four straight wins. They still lead the Central at 6-4 over Hold Timers on points, and their odds of winning the division went from 60% to 66%. The Magi have lost five straight and their playoff odds are down to 2%. The good news for them is in the draft: they move from the 4th pick to the 3rd.

### Mailing It In 138.7, Sour Grapes 112.2

Jaxon Smith-Njigba 31.1, Deebo Samuel 28.0. Mailing It In end a two-game skid and take first in the West and the 2 seed from Fully Cooked. Their playoff odds went from 85% to 91%. Sour Grapes got 34.4 from Dak Prescott and 34.2 from Chuba Hubbard and still lost by 26.5. C.J. Stroud put up 20.4 on the bench again. Sour Grapes slide from 3rd to 4th in the draft order.

### Wholey Club 102.8, Son of Tinactin 83.0

Stefon Diggs 20.3, Matthew Stafford 19.8. The Draft Bowl went to Wholey Club, who have won two straight and are 5-5. They still hold the 1.01. Son of Tinactin stay at 2, and they left George Pickens' 43.5 on the bench. That's the third-best score by anyone in the league this week, and it didn't count.

### Bullish on da Bears 108.8, Jesus Christ College 89.3

Ja'Marr Chase 34.1, Brock Purdy 31.7. Bullish are 8-2 with four straight wins, and their bye odds went from 66% to 76%. JCC are 2-8 and stay at 5 in the draft. They also own Bullish's first, which is still projected 12th.

## Awards watch

**On a record pace.** Smith-Njigba leads the MVP race with 263.2 points, and the site has him on pace for 342.1. The single-season record for a wide receiver is Cooper Kupp's 335.4 from 2021. Gibbs is second in the MVP race with 253.4 and on pace for 366.2. The running back record is 354.1, set in 2019 by Christian McCaffrey. So the man who just had the best game of the season is also the one whose record Gibbs is chasing.

**All-FBP, rising:** McCaffrey's 46.3 moved him onto the first team at running back. The Vikings defense (Thots) took over the first-team defense spot from Denver. James Cook III (Fully Cooked) joined the second team, and Amon-Ra St. Brown is back on it.

**All-FBP, falling:** McCaffrey's jump bumped Kenneth Walker III to the second team. Nacua (IR) and Javonte Williams dropped off the list.

**Where's Burrow?** People keep asking me why Joe Cool isn't on an All-FBP team. Burrow has 232.4 points this season, the most of any quarterback in the league. But All-FBP counts starter points, and Sean M. didn't start him in Weeks 2 through 4. Jalen Hurts and Tyler Shough got those starts while Burrow put up 62.5 points on the bench. Add the Week 6 bye and Burrow has 169.9 starter points. Dak Prescott holds the second-team spot with 201.8. Burrow's closing fast, though. He's averaged 32.8 over his last four starts. At that rate he catches Prescott in two or three weeks.

**Introducing the All-FBP Rookie Team.** It's new this year. The Rookie Team counts every point a rookie scores, whether he's starting, on the bench or on the taxi squad. Plenty of good rookies spend the season on taxi on purpose to keep them cheap, and the regular All-FBP teams never see them. You'll know them on the site by the green ring on their photos. Rookie of the Year now counts the same way.

This year's team: Fernando Mendoza at QB, Emmett Johnson and Seth McGowan at RB, Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion at WR, Kenyon Sadiq at TE and Carnell Tate at flex. Son of Tinactin have three of the seven. Hold Timers have two.

**A new Rookie of the Year leader.** Emmett Johnson scored 35.1 this week and went from fourth to first. He has 112.0 points, ahead of Boston's 108.2. Johnson was the 2.06 pick, he makes $3, and every one of those points came on Hold Timers' taxi squad. That raises a question for Ronnie K. Does he get called up? Hold Timers just won a playoff fight with a running back committee. Promoting Johnson now gets them a back who's scored 73.5 over the last three weeks. The cost is in the rulebook: his salary escalators start early and he gives up the sixth-year option. I wouldn't want to make that call.

**Manager of the Year.** It's not just record. The site combines a few different measures into one score to find the team that's been run the best. Bullish lead, and Hold Timers moved up from third to second. Mailing It In joined the top three, and Fully Cooked dropped out.
