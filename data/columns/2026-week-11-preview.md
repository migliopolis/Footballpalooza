Six NFL teams are off this week: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Rams, New England and Seattle. No FBP team loses more points to it than Mailing It In, the team in first place in the West.

A word on the win chances below. They come from the site's computer, which knows how well your team has played. It doesn't know who's on bye.

### Mailing It In (6-3-1) at The Fist of the Magi (4-6)

The computer likes Mailing at 65%. It hasn't looked at the roster. Jaxon Smith-Njigba, the MVP leader, is off. So is Bijan Robinson. That's 460.9 points between them. Mailing also carry two defenses, Green Bay and Cleveland, and both are on bye.

Wednesday's waivers fixed part of that. Mailing claimed the Chargers defense, projected for 12.3, and receiver Elic Ayomanor, projected for 10.0. Their best lineup now comes to 110.4. The Magi's best is 109.2. Yes, those Magi, losers of five straight at 69.6 a game over the last three.

Fully Cooked had the same problem, with New England as their only defense. They went and got the Jets.

### Hold Timers (6-4) at Bullish on da Bears (8-2)

No game moves more playoff odds. Hold Timers hold the 5 seed at 70%, and they go to 82% with a win or 52% with a loss. Bullish are playing for the bye: 95% with a win, 63% with a loss.

The computer has the 1 seed as a 42% underdog in its own building. It made them an underdog in Week 9 too, and Bullish won by 35.5. My old man would have had a word for that computer. I'll leave it there.

The projections have it 121.7 to 119.7, Hold Timers. Bullish have won four straight and lead the series 6-3, but Brock Bowers is questionable with an ankle. Hold Timers lose Drake London and Blake Corum to byes. Their best flex is Xavier Hutchinson, claimed Wednesday and projected for 9.1, while Emmett Johnson sits on the taxi squad at 11.2. I asked Tuesday if Ronnie K. calls him up. A game this close makes it a harder question.

## Around the league

**Hauk Tua (5-5) at Jesus Christ College (2-8).** Hauk Tua are the first team out: 55% with a win, 28% with a loss. Drake Maye is off, so it's Kirk Cousins. JCC have it worse. They cut Deshaun Watson on Wednesday, a week after claiming him. Malik Willis and Jaxson Dart are on IR, and the best quarterback left is Cade Klubnik, projected for 4.6.

**Thots & Players (5-5) at Son of Tinactin (3-6-1).** Thots project for 130.8 and have an 82% chance. Tinactin play without Jordan Love. They also own Mailing's first-round pick, so they'll have an eye on the Magi game.

**Fully Cooked (6-4) at Wholey Club (5-5).** Cooked are an 84% favorite with the week's top projection, 131.9, though that counts on Kenneth Walker III, who's questionable with a groin injury. Wholey have the fewest points in the league, two straight wins and a 5-2 lead in this series. They also own Cooked's first-round pick, and that pick gets better if Cooked slide.

**Sour Grapes (3-7) at MR. FROBOT (6-4).** FROBOT have won four straight and five in a row in this series, and they're a 79% favorite. Kyren Williams and Harold Fannin Jr. are on bye, so they spent $4 on Mike Gesicki to play tight end.

One more thing. The trade deadline is Thursday, December 3, at 12 pm ET. That's two Sundays from now. Weeks like this one show you how thin you are, and Jonathan Taylor, Nico Collins and Bucky Irving are all sitting on the trading block.

See you after the games. Bring the coffee.
