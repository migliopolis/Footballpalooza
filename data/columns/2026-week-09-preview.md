Last week I told you the West was fine. Then the West went 0-4. So take this with whatever you take your coffee with: halfway through the season, eight teams have a real shot at six playoff spots, and four of them live in the Central, all within a game of each other.

This week the 1 seed plays a road game it's expected to lose, the defending champs get a team that has nothing to lose, and the Magi get the league's lowest scorer, which happens to be the one team that's beaten them six times. Let's go to the board.

## The matchups

Win chances come from the site's computer, which plays out the rest of the season 20,000 times. Series records count every meeting since 2019, playoffs included.

### Hauk Tua (4-4) at Mailing It In (5-2-1) — game of the week

No game on the slate moves more playoff odds. Hauk Tua sit at 42% and can go to 58% with a win or 29% with a loss. That's a 28-point swing on one Sunday. Mailing goes to 93% with a win and 74% with a loss.

The computer has Mailing a 55% favorite, about as close to a coin flip as it gets. The series is 4-4, but Mailing has won the last three, including a round-one playoff game in 2024 by 55. Hauk Tua put up 153.6 last week, the best score in the league, and left 28.2 from Kirk Cousins on the bench while they did it. The champs just lost on Monday night by 3.6. If you're picking an upset, this is where I'd look.

### Bullish on da Bears (6-2) at Thots & Players (4-4)

The 1 seed is a 2-to-1 underdog. Thots are a 67% favorite, and I understand why: 1,022.3 points, second in the league, and an all-play record of 58-30. Bullish are eighth in points at 853.3, with an all-play record of 39-49. Eight weeks in, the computer still doesn't believe in them.

But Bullish just scored 146.8 with Ja'Marr Chase in the lineup, and they're 95% to make the playoffs either way. What they're playing for is the bye: 61% with a win, 27% with a loss. Thots are playing for their footing. They've lost two straight, and a loss drops them to 72% to make it and 4% for a bye. These two have met three times in the playoffs. Thots lead the series 6-5.

### Wholey Club (3-5) at The Fist of the Magi (4-4)

The Magi are a 72% favorite, and they need every bit of it. Three straight losses took them from first in the Central to last, and they've averaged 77.7 over that stretch. Win and they're back to 45%; lose and they're at 20%.

Here's the problem. Wholey Club owns this series 6-2 and has won the last two. Wholey is last in points, at 726.2, and averaging 79.7 over its last three, so neither team is scoring right now. Last week I said that if the Magi can't win this one, I'll stop calling them a contender. I'm not taking it back.

### Hold Timers (5-3) at Sour Grapes (2-6)

The new Central leader is a 67% favorite and has won three straight, averaging 128.9 in that stretch. Hold Timers lead the series 5-3. A win puts them at 77% to make the playoffs and 38% for a bye. A loss drops them to 50%. In a division this tight, there's no such thing as a safe Sunday. Sour Grapes are at 0.2%, and this is mostly about pride.

### Son of Tinactin (3-4-1) at MR. FROBOT (4-4)

FROBOT is the league's top scorer at 1,041.1, 131.0 a game over the last three, and an 80% favorite. They've beaten Son of Tinactin seven straight times and lead the series 8-1. A win puts them at 91% to make the playoffs; a loss, 76%. SOT is at 5%, 15% with a win.

### Jesus Christ College (2-6) at Fully Cooked (5-3)

Fully Cooked is a 74% favorite, and nobody in the league has better bye odds, at 40.5%. That goes to 48% with a win and 21% with a loss. Their all-play record of 63-25 is the best in the league. JCC is coming off its second win of the year, and here's one for you: JCC leads this series 7-4.

## Before I go

- **Upset watch:** Bullish over Thots (33%). It's not an upset when the 1 seed wins. It just looks like one on paper.
- **Biggest swing:** Hauk Tua's playoff odds move 28 points on one game. The Magi's move 25, and Hold Timers' move 27.
- **Points vs. record:** FROBOT and Thots are first and second in scoring, and both are 4-4. Bullish is 6-2 on the eighth-most points. I keep saying something has to give. It hasn't yet.
- **Lowest bar:** Wholey and the Magi have averaged a combined 157.4 points over their last three weeks. One of them wins Sunday anyway.
