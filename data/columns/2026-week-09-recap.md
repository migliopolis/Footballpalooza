It's a tale of two franchises. The best team this league has had since it went PPR is back where it usually is. The worst team this league has had since it went PPR is in first place. If you had both of those on your card in September, you're lying.

## FROBOT are back

**MR. FROBOT started 2-4. They're now leading the Central.** Three straight wins: 139.3, 153.4 and 151.6 this week, the top score in the league. That's 148 a game over three weeks. They have the most points in the league (1,192.7), the No. 1 power ranking, and the 3 seed. Three teams are 5-4 in the Central. FROBOT hold the tiebreak on total points, and it isn't close.

None of this should surprise anyone. From 2019 through last year, FROBOT went 71-29-1. That's a .703 winning percentage, almost 90 points better than anybody else's. They finished first in the regular season three times, went 13-1 in 2024, and won the title in 2023. Last year's 8-5 counted as a down year for them. The slow start this year was the strange part. This is just them getting back to normal.

The engine is the Cincinnati stack. Joe Burrow has 39.5 and 29.5 the last two weeks. Tee Higgins added 20.2 this week. And CeeDee Lamb, who doesn't even play in Cincinnati, put up 33.5. The computer has FROBOT's playoff chances up from 88% to 96%, and their odds of winning the Central from 38% to 60%.

## Fully Cooked, of all teams

**Fully Cooked are 6-3 and alone in first place in the West.** Read that twice. From 2019 through 2025, this franchise went 37-64. That's the worst record in the league over that stretch. They've never had a winning season in the PPR era; their best was 7-7 in 2024. They finished last in 2023, and they're one of three teams that haven't played in a title game since 2019.

I went back through the weekly standings as far as they go, to 2019. Fully Cooked have never been in first place by themselves this late in a season. The closest they came was 2022, when they were tied for the West lead after Week 9 and lost the tiebreak on points. The only other time they had first place to themselves was the Week 8 standings in 2019, and that was at 4-4.

This year they've earned it. Fully Cooked have the most all-play wins in the league at 72-26, meaning they'd have beaten everybody else that week in 72 of 98 tries. They're second in points. They hold the 2 seed, and the computer gives them a 61% chance at a bye and a 71% chance to win the division. Two more wins and they've clinched the first winning season in franchise history. Five games left. Next up is Thots & Players, and that game is close to a coin flip.

## Welcome to the FBP Least

**Bullish on da Bears are 7-2 and three games up on the rest of the East.** I'd call it a division race, but there's nobody racing. Wholey Club, Son of Tinactin and Jesus Christ College are a combined 9-17-1. Bullish are 3-0 against them, and the computer has them at 99.5% to win the division.

They're still not a great team on paper. Bullish are seventh in points and have a losing all-play record (48-50). But Ja'Marr Chase has put up 31.1 and 31.5 in his first two weeks, and Bullish have scored 146.8 and 146.7 since he showed up. They walked into Thots' building as a 2-to-1 underdog this week and won by 35.

## The games

### MR. FROBOT 151.6, Son of Tinactin 83.3

Lamb 33.5, Burrow 29.5, Javonte Williams 22.4, Higgins 20.2. Jordan Love put up 36.2 for Son of Tinactin, and he got no help: nobody else on the team cracked 15. Tinactin also left Bryce Young's 20.3 on the bench.

### Fully Cooked 146.7, Jesus Christ College 82.6

Kenneth Walker III scored 36.3, the most by any starter this week. Puka Nacua added 35.0. Between them, those two outscored JCC's top five starters combined. JCC are 2-7 and five games back in the East.

### Bullish on da Bears 146.7, Thots & Players 111.2

Chase 31.5, Brock Purdy 25.3, Brock Bowers 22.3. Bullish even left Shedeur Sanders' 22.7 on the bench. Matthew Golden put up 32.4 for Thots, but Josh Allen gave them 12.9 and Tucker Kraft 1.9. That's three straight losses for Thots.

### Hauk Tua 121.8, Mailing It In 103.3

In my preview I said Hauk Tua's season was on the line. They answered. Christian Watson scored 30.1 and the 49ers defense 23.0. Bijan Robinson put up 26.7 for the champs, but Jaxon Smith-Njigba was held to 12.1. Mailing have lost two straight and slipped from the 2 seed to the 4.

### Sour Grapes 93.9, Hold Timers 82.5

Bucky Irving 28.5, Dak Prescott 19.5. Sour Grapes also left C.J. Stroud's 35.4 on the bench, again. Meanwhile Hold Timers sat the Chicago defense (20.0) and Travis Kelce (17.3) and started D'Andre Swift, who gave them 3.1. Their best possible lineup would have scored 110.3 and won. Instead they dropped out of the playoff picture.

### Wholey Club 64.5, The Fist of the Magi 56.6

The lowest-scoring game of the year. Wholey Club's best player was the Jacksonville defense, again, with 15.0. The Magi got 0.0 from Ladd McConkey, who didn't play, and 0.5 from Josh Jacobs. Last week I said that if the Magi couldn't beat Wholey Club, I'd stop calling them a contender. They couldn't. Four straight losses, and their playoff chances are down to 14%.
