Three teams are 5-4 in the Central. Only one of them is in first, and the other two play each other on Sunday. That's the game. Everything else on this week's card is a sideshow, and one of those sideshows is the West lead.

Win chances come from the site's computer, which plays out the rest of the season 20,000 times. Series records count every meeting since 2019, playoffs included.

## Hold Timers (5-4) at Hauk Tua (5-4) — game of the week

Hauk Tua hold the 5 seed. Hold Timers are the first team out. Hold Timers' playoff odds go from 49% to 70% with a win or 30% with a loss; Hauk Tua's go from 67% to 84% or 47%. That's 77.6 points of playoff odds riding on one division game. No other game this week moves more than 24.5.

The computer has Hauk Tua a 53% favorite, and the projections have them up 121.0 to 114.0. Blame the bye list: Hold Timers lose D'Andre Swift, Bo Nix and the Bears defense. Hauk Tua only lose Colston Loveland.

Then there's Detroit. Hauk Tua's best player is Jahmyr Gibbs, with 209.2 points, the most by any running back in the league. Hold Timers' best player is his teammate Amon-Ra St. Brown, at 159.4, and he's questionable with a rib injury. Whatever the Lions do on Sunday shows up on both sides of this box score. Hauk Tua have worries too: Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot) didn't practice Wednesday, and the Vikings won't commit to Kirk Cousins as their starter after trading for him.

Hauk Tua have the form, two straight wins and 131.6 a game over their last three, and the history, 7-4 in the series with the last two. Hold Timers have the 2019 semifinal, the only playoff meeting, which they won 153.0 to 143.4. That's not much, but it's something.

## Thots & Players (4-5) at Fully Cooked (6-3)

Fully Cooked lead the West. Thots have lost three straight. And the projections still have Thots ahead, 129.5 to 128.9. The computer calls it 52-48 for Cooked.

Cooked are at 99% to make the playoffs either way. The bye is what's at stake: 78% with a win, 43% with a loss. A win also gets them to 7-3, one victory from their first winning season since the league went PPR. Thots are at 88%, 82% with a loss, and they're too good a team (third in points, 65-34 all-play) to stay on a slide like this.

The trouble for Cooked is the series. Thots lead it 9-4 and have won four straight, including 157.7 to 153.7 in Week 4. And Cooked play this one without Puka Nacua, who's out until mid-December with a knee injury. Thots are missing David Montgomery (questionable) and Rome Odunze (bye), but they have Josh Allen, projected for 26.8.

## Around the league

**The Fist of the Magi (4-5) at MR. FROBOT (5-4).** FROBOT are a 69% favorite, have won three straight and lead the league in points. The Magi have lost four straight and averaged 67.3 over their last three, the lowest in the league by almost nine points. FROBOT lead the series 12-3, but the Magi won both meetings last year.

**Mailing It In (5-3-1) at Sour Grapes (3-6).** The champs have lost two straight and are a 71% favorite. Sour Grapes just knocked off Hold Timers and lead the series 6-5.

**Son of Tinactin (3-5-1) at Wholey Club (4-5).** Call it the Draft Bowl. These two are sitting on the 1.01 and 1.02. The draft order runs on potential points, not wins, so whichever team has the bigger week slides toward 1.02, win or lose.

**Bullish on da Bears (7-2) at Jesus Christ College (2-7).** Bullish are a 63% favorite and playing for the bye: 79% with a win, 45% with a loss. JCC lose four starters to byes and are projected for 60.7. They also own Bullish's first-round pick, so a JCC win would pay twice.

## What it means

The Central sends one team for sure, and maybe two. The winner in Hauk Tua's building is in good shape. The loser needs help. In the West, if Fully Cooked lose and Mailing It In win, Mailing take over first. And the two byes are up for grabs: Bullish and Fully Cooked hold them today, and both lose a lot of that grip with a loss.

See you after the games. Bring the coffee.
