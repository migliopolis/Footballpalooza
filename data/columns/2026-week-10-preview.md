Three teams are 5-4 in the Central. Only one of them is in first, and the other two play each other on Sunday. That's the game. Everything else on this week's card is a sideshow, and one of those sideshows is the West lead.

Five weeks left. Let's go to the board.

## The matchups

Win chances come from the site's computer, which plays out the rest of the season 20,000 times. Series records count every meeting since 2019, playoffs included.

### Hold Timers (5-4) at Hauk Tua (5-4) — game of the week

No game on this week's card comes close. Hold Timers sit at 49% to make the playoffs. A win puts them at 70%; a loss drops them to 30%. Hauk Tua sit at 67% and go to 84% with a win or 47% with a loss. Add the two swings together and you get 77.6 points of playoff odds riding on one division game. The next-biggest game this week moves 24.5.

Here's where they stand. FROBOT lead the Central on total points. Hauk Tua hold the 5 seed, a wild card, on the same tiebreak. Hold Timers are the first team out. Win on Sunday and you're in the picture. Lose and you're looking up at it, and in Hold Timers' case, looking at a lottery pick: they'd be on the clock at 1.06 if the season ended today.

The computer has Hauk Tua a 53% favorite, and the projections have them ahead 121.0 to 114.0. The reason for that gap is on the bye list. Hold Timers lose D'Andre Swift (153.8 points, second on the team), Bo Nix (130.8) and the Bears defense (114.0) this week. Trevor Lawrence covers the quarterback spot, but that's a lot of lineup sitting at home. Hauk Tua only lose Colston Loveland.

Then there's the Detroit problem. Hauk Tua's best player is Jahmyr Gibbs, with 209.2 points, the most by any running back in the league and second overall to Jaxon Smith-Njigba. Hold Timers' best player is Amon-Ra St. Brown, at 159.4. They share a huddle in Detroit, so whatever the Lions do on Sunday shows up on both sides of this box score. St. Brown (ribs) was limited in practice Wednesday and is questionable. Keep an eye on that one.

Hauk Tua have their own worries. Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot), who's averaged 21.2 over his last three, didn't practice Wednesday. And Kirk Cousins, who scored 172.2 points before the Vikings traded for him on Tuesday, has a new head coach who won't yet say he's starting in Week 10. Hauk Tua have Drake Maye either way, so the quarterback spot is covered.

Form says Hauk Tua. They've won two straight, beat Mailing It In 121.8 to 103.3 last week behind 30.1 from Christian Watson, and have averaged 131.6 over their last three. Hold Timers lost to 2-6 Sour Grapes, 93.9 to 82.5, their lowest score of the season, and have averaged 105.9 over the same stretch.

History says Hauk Tua too. They lead the series 7-4 and have won the last two, including 91.8 to 89.2 in Week 4. That was a 2.6-point game. But the only time these two met in the playoffs, the 2019 semifinal, Hold Timers won 153.0 to 143.4. If you're looking for a reason to pick the underdog, there it is. I'm not sure it's enough.

### Thots & Players (4-5) at Fully Cooked (6-3)

This is the strangest close game on the board. Thots have lost three straight and sit as the 6 seed, holding it on all-play wins. Fully Cooked lead the West. And yet the projections have Thots ahead, 129.5 to 128.9. The computer still gives Cooked the edge at 52%, a coin flip with a little thumb on the scale.

What's at stake for Fully Cooked isn't the playoffs. They're at 99% either way. It's the bye: 78% with a win, 43% with a loss. That's 35.6 points on one game, the biggest bye swing on the slate. A win also puts them at 7-3, one victory from the first winning season this franchise has had since the league went PPR in 2019.

What's at stake for Thots is their spot. They're at 88% to make it, 93% with a win and 82% with a loss, and that loss would mean four straight. They have the third-most points in the league (1,133.5) and an all-play record of 65-34, so they're a good team on a bad run.

And here's the part Fully Cooked won't like. Thots own this series 9-4 and have won four straight, including 157.7 to 153.7 in Week 4. Last year in Week 10 they won by 64.3.

Fully Cooked do this one without Puka Nacua. Their second-leading scorer (173.6 points, 27.3 a game over his last three) is out with a knee injury and isn't expected back until mid-December. Patrick Mahomes (elbow, knee) is listed as questionable but practiced fully Wednesday. T.J. Hockenson (wrist, chest) didn't practice at all. Kenneth Walker, who put up 36.3 last week, carries the load.

Thots have their own holes: David Montgomery (groin) is questionable, Kyle Monangai (toe) is out, and Rome Odunze is on bye. But Josh Allen is projected for 26.8, the best on either side, and Christian McCaffrey has averaged 25.1 over his last three.

One more thing. If Fully Cooked lose and Mailing It In win at Sour Grapes, Mailing take over first in the West.

### The Fist of the Magi (4-5) at MR. FROBOT (5-4)

FROBOT are a 69% favorite and they've earned it: three straight wins, 148.1 a game over that stretch, and the most points in the league (1,192.7). A win puts them at 98% to make it and 40% for a bye.

The Magi have lost four straight and averaged 67.3 over their last three weeks. That's the lowest number in the league by almost nine points. They're at 14%. A win gets them to 26%; a loss, 8%. FROBOT lead the series 12-3 and beat the Magi for the title in 2023, but the Magi won both meetings last year. That's the only good news I have for them.

### Mailing It In (5-3-1) at Sour Grapes (3-6)

The champs have lost two straight and are a 71% favorite here. A win puts them at 90%; a loss, 73%. Sour Grapes lead the series 6-5 and just knocked off Hold Timers, so don't sleep on them. They'd still need a lot more than one win: 0.3% to make the playoffs, 1% with a win.

### Son of Tinactin (3-5-1) at Wholey Club (4-5)

Call it the Draft Bowl. These two are sitting on the top two picks, and they play each other. Neither team's playoff odds get past 4% either way, so this one is about the draft board. More on that below.

### Bullish on da Bears (7-2) at Jesus Christ College (2-7)

The 1 seed is a 63% favorite, and the bye is the prize: 79% with a win, 45% with a loss. Bullish have won three straight, averaging 132.2. JCC lose Luther Burden, Jaylen Waddle, Emeka Egbuka and DeVonta Smith to byes and are projected for 60.7, the lowest on the board. Here's the twist: JCC own Bullish's first-round pick. Beating Bullish is the one win that pays JCC twice, because every loss Bullish takes moves that pick up the board. These two met in the 2022 title game, and Bullish won it 113.2 to 90.2.
