I've seen a lot of Week 8s. Most of them sort out a couple of things and leave the rest for November. This one turned over the whole board. A division leader is now in last place. A .500 team has more points than anybody. And the West went 0-4 against the rest of the league in a single weekend.

## The Central flips

**The Fist of the Magi led the Central all season. They're now in last place.** Three straight losses will do that. They scored 88.0, then 74.0, then 71.2 this week, the lowest score in the league. A week ago they held the 3 seed. Today they're on the outside of the playoff picture, and the computer has their playoff chances down from 73% to 38%.

**Hold Timers took their spot.** Three straight wins and first place in the Central at 5-3. They needed Monday night to get there: D'Andre Swift put up 27.9 for Chicago to beat the defending champs by 3.6.

**MR. FROBOT is the best team nobody's talking about.** They're 4-4, but they've scored 131 a game over their last three, and their 1,041.1 points are now the most in the league. They hold the 5 seed. All four teams in the Central are within one game of each other, so this division won't be settled for weeks.

## The East has a favorite

Bullish on da Bears are 6-2, they hold the 1 seed, and the computer gives them a 95% chance to win the East. Nobody else in that division is above .500.

Bullish have been winning ugly all year: eighth in points, with an all-play record of 39-49. That's why they went and got Ja'Marr Chase, and in his first week he put up 31.1. Bullish scored 146.8, their second-best total of the season. If Chase gives them 25 a week, Bullish stop being the team that wins close and start being the team you don't want to see in December.

## The West's lost weekend

All four West teams played someone from another division this week. All four lost. Thots & Players and Sour Grapes have now lost two straight, and Mailing It In and Fully Cooked one each. That's six straight losses across the division.

The West still holds the 2, 4 and 6 seeds, so nobody's panicking yet. But Thots slipped from the 5 seed to the 6, and Mailing lost their hold on the 1 seed to Bullish.

## The games

### Hold Timers 106.1, Mailing It In 102.5

The game of the week came down to Monday night, with Seattle's Jaxon Smith-Njigba for the champs against Chicago's D'Andre Swift for Hold Timers. Smith-Njigba put up 23.8. Swift put up 27.9. Tony Pollard led Mailing with 28.7, but Hold Timers got the win, the division lead and a three-game winning streak.

### Bullish on da Bears 146.8, Fully Cooked 123.4

Chase 31.1, Brock Bowers 28.2, Michael Wilson 26.8. Fully Cooked got 25.4 from Patrick Mahomes and 25.0 from Puka Nacua and still lost by 23. Chase alone outscored every Fully Cooked starter.

### Hauk Tua 153.6, Sour Grapes 117.6

The top score of the week. Bhayshul Tuten put up 29.9 and Jahmyr Gibbs 24.8. Hauk Tua even left Kirk Cousins' 28.2 on the bench and still won by 36. They're 4-4 and right back in the Central race.

### MR. FROBOT 153.4, Wholey Club 93.6

Joe Burrow scored 39.5, the most by any player this week, and Tee Higgins added 37.5. Two players, 77 points. Wholey Club's best player was the Jacksonville defense.

### Jesus Christ College 118.4, Thots & Players 102.3

Tyler Allgeier put up 36.1 for JCC's second win of the year. Thots got 26.6 from Josh Allen, while Cam Ward put up 26.3 on their bench. With one QB slot, there was nowhere to put him. Next year, there will be.

### Son of Tinactin 97.4, The Fist of the Magi 71.2

Josh Jacobs scored 23.2 for the Magi. Nobody else on the team cracked 15. Son of Tinactin won without their best two scores of the week: Adonai Mitchell's 27.2 and Jordan Love's 21.5 both sat on the bench.

## Next week

Bullish and Thots & Players meet in Week 9, the 1 seed against a team that's lost two straight and needs a win. The Magi get Wholey Club, about as soft a landing as there is. If they can't win that one, I'll stop calling them a contender.
