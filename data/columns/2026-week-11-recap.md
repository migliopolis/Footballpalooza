Sour Grapes did everything right this week. They set a near-perfect lineup, got 35.9 from their quarterback, and had the best score in the league with one game left to play. Then Joe Burrow got one last chance on Monday night, and it belonged to the team that always seems to get it.

## FROBOT steal one

### MR. FROBOT 136.3, Sour Grapes 133.4

Going into Monday night, Sour Grapes were sitting on 133.4. That was the top score of Week 11 at the time, and it wasn't a fluke. Dak Prescott put up 35.9, Chuba Hubbard 26.0, Bucky Irving 19.0 and the Ravens defense 17.0. Ken N. even got the lineup right. Their best possible score was 133.6, so he left two-tenths of a point on the table. C.J. Stroud's 20.5 sat on the bench again, but this time it didn't matter. Nobody beats 133.4 by accident.

FROBOT had already gotten 30.2 from CeeDee Lamb and 24.1 from Javonte Williams. What they still had was the Cincinnati stack, playing in Washington on Monday night: Joe Burrow, Tee Higgins, and Mike Gesicki, the $4 tight end they picked up for this week to cover for Kyren Williams and Harold Fannin Jr. on bye.

Here's how it went. Washington kicked a field goal with 2:26 left to go up 19-17. That was Burrow's last chance to win it for FROBOT, and he did it on back-to-back throws to the two FROBOT pass catchers on the field: 12 yards to Higgins, then 32 to Gesicki. The Bengals got to the Washington 38 with 51 seconds left. Burrow got sacked on third down, and Cincinnati settled for a 56-yard field goal and a 20-19 lead. That drive was worth about eight points to FROBOT: two to Higgins, four to Gesicki, close to two more for Burrow's passing yards. FROBOT won by 2.9.

Washington went 64 yards in four plays after that and kicked the winner. The Bengals lost the real game, 22-20. FROBOT won ours. Sour Grapes have one more thing to be sore about: their taxi-squad rookie, Antonio Williams, got the headlines for Washington's win the next morning. Taxi points don't count.

He can, and he does. I told you two weeks ago this was coming. FROBOT started 2-4. They've won five straight since, scored 717.3 over those five weeks, and lead the league in points with 1,465.7. They hold the Central on points over Hold Timers, they move up to the 2 seed, and their odds of a first-round bye went from 37% to 52%. That's six straight wins over Sour Grapes, too. Nobody in this league has won more since 2011, and this is what it looks like when they do it. They don't need your sympathy. They'd like your lunch money.

FROBOT's flex had a good week on the awards page, too. Javonte Williams moved onto the All-FBP 2nd Team. And Lamb is third in the MVP race with 252.2.

Sour Grapes get something out of it besides heartbreak. Prescott's 35.9 moved him up to the All-FBP 1st Team at quarterback. He has 237.7 starter points, and he passed Josh Allen to get there. At 3-8, Sour Grapes also slip from the 4th pick to the 5th.

## Hold Timers stay hot, Bullish cool off

### Hold Timers 101.4, Bullish on da Bears 87.5

Bullish came in 8-2 on a four-game winning streak. Hold Timers came in winners of three of their last four. Only one of those streaks survived.

Travis Kelce led Hold Timers with 25.4, the Bears defense put up 18.0, and Amon-Ra St. Brown added 14.7. It wasn't pretty, but it was enough, and that's four wins in five weeks for Hold Timers. They also left Jack Bech's 22.8 on the bench. Ronnie K. can live with that one.

Bullish can't say the same. Their bench had Mike Evans with 22.9 and Brock Purdy with 22.2. They started Pat Bryant (6.2) and Caleb Williams (14.3) instead. Their best possible lineup was 112.6, which beats 101.4. Ja'Marr Chase only gave them 8.1, though his season was good enough to move him up to the All-FBP 1st Team at flex. The Texans defense, their 18.0 top scorer this week, joined the 2nd Team.

Bullish are all but in, 100% in the computer's simulation, and they're still the 1 seed at 8-3. But the bye isn't the lock it looked like a week ago. Their bye odds dropped from 76% to 62%, and their chances at the 1 seed from 56% to 37%. They go to Hauk Tua next, and the computer makes them a 40% underdog. Bullish have scored 87.5 and 108.8 the last two weeks. That's cooling off.

Hold Timers went from 70% to 80% to make the playoffs, the biggest jump of the week, and they move up to the 4 seed at 7-4. Their win also moved their draft pick from 8th to 9th, which they'll happily take.

## The rest of the card

### Hauk Tua 98.4, Jesus Christ College 37.0

Start with the record. JCC's 37.0 is the lowest score in any FBP game since 2019. The old mark was 43.7, by Sour Grapes in 2022. Their starting quarterback, Jalon Daniels, scored 0.0. The Dolphins defense was their top scorer at 11.0, and nobody else reached 10. Their best possible lineup would have scored 50.9. Something to build on. At 2-9, they jump from the 5th pick to the 3rd, and they still own Bullish's first.

Jahmyr Gibbs put up 27.8 for Hauk Tua and took the MVP lead. He has 281.2 starter points, ahead of Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 263.2, who didn't score this week. Gibbs is on pace for 365.5, which would break Christian McCaffrey's running back record of 354.1. That's two-thirds of the Triple Crown we wrote about last week, MVP and the All-FBP 1st Team, both in hand for now. The third part is the hard part. Hauk Tua are 6-5 and 7th, and their playoff odds slipped from 48% to 44% even with the win. No title, no crown.

### Fully Cooked 105.5, Wholey Club 83.9

Patrick Mahomes 25.0 and Malik Nabers 18.5. Fully Cooked move up to the 3 seed and lead the West, and their odds of winning the division went from 54% to 70%. That's not great news for Wholey Club, who own Fully Cooked's first-round pick. It slides from 9th to 10th. Wholey Club still hold the 1st pick with their own.

### The Fist of the Magi 122.1, Mailing It In 98.0

Ashton Jeanty 27.9, Jared Goff 20.4, and the Broncos defense 19.0, which put Denver back on the All-FBP 1st Team. Mailing It In played without Smith-Njigba and Bijan Robinson, who both put up zeros on the bench, and Bijan slipped to the All-FBP 2nd Team. The loss cost Mailing a lot. They fall from the 2 seed to the 5th, and their bye odds went from 25% to 9%. Son of Tinactin own Mailing's first, and it moves up from 11th to 8th. The Magi are 5-6 and drop from the 3rd pick to the 4th. Their playoff odds are 3%.

### Thots & Players 94.9, Son of Tinactin 78.5

Tetairoa McMillan 19.1, Zay Flowers 16.4. Thots won, but they left Cam Ward's 22.2 and Baker Mayfield's 19.1 on the bench, and Josh Allen gave them 12.2. That cost Allen his All-FBP 1st Team spot. He's down to the 2nd Team behind Prescott, by 2.4 points. Thots hold the 6 seed on all-play wins. Son of Tinactin have lost three straight and stay at 2 in the draft.
