Nine days before the Dec 3 deadline, we have a trade. It went through Tuesday morning, and the timing is the first thing you notice: these two teams play each other this week.

- **Fully Cooked get:** WR George Pickens (DAL), $24, year 2 of 3
- **Son of Tinactin get:** WR Denzel Boston (CLE), $5, rookie, year 1 of 5

## Fully Cooked: the stretch-run receiver

Pickens has 171.6 points in 11 games, 14.8 a game, and he's averaging 9.0 targets a game in Dallas. His Trade Value is 5,735. Boston's is 4,279, so on paper Fully Cooked paid a fair price for the better player right now.

Fully Cooked needed him. Puka Nacua is on IR. Malik Nabers (155.7) and Nico Collins (130.8) are fine, but after them it was DK Metcalf at 94.5. Pickens slots in as a top-two receiver for a 7-4 team with a 99.3 percent chance at the playoffs and roughly even odds at a bye.

Two things to know. First, Dallas has its bye in Week 14, the last week of the regular season, so Pickens can't help in the final push for seeding. Fully Cooked play Mailing It In that week. He's back for the playoffs. Second, he costs $30 next year in the last year of his deal. Our keeper model gives him a 54 percent chance of being kept at that price. Fully Cooked are buying a rental with an option.

## Son of Tinactin: younger, cheaper, and maybe a better pick

Boston was Fully Cooked's 1.11 in this year's rookie draft and he's on the All-FBP Rookie Team: 108.2 points in 10 games, 10.8 a game, as a rookie. He's $5 this year and runs $5, $8, $14, $23 and $35 over the five years of his deal. Son of Tinactin keep him for years at a price Pickens will never see again.

Then there's the draft angle. Son of Tinactin are 3-7-1 and out of the playoff race. As Lainey laid out Monday, they're second in the draft order, 21.9 potential points behind Wholey Club for the 1.01. Potential points count the best lineup a team could have set. Take Son of Tinactin's best receiver off the roster, and that number grows more slowly for the last three weeks. Whether that was the plan or not, it helps them chase Jeremiah Smith. Fittingly, Pickens scored 43.5 in Week 10 while sitting on their bench.

## The Week 12 wrinkle

Fully Cooked and Son of Tinactin play each other this week. Fully Cooked were already 83 percent favorites. Now Pickens lines up against the team that just traded him. Son of Tinactin won't mind a loss. Fully Cooked won't mind a bigger margin.

## Grades

**Fully Cooked: B+.** They got the best player in the deal for a title run and paid with a rookie they could afford to move. The Week 14 bye and a coin-flip keeper decision keep it from an A.

**Son of Tinactin: A-.** A non-contender turned a 25-year-old who's about to cost $30 into a 22-year-old on a five-year rookie deal, and may have improved their draft position in the process. That's how you sell.
