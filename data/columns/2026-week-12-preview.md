For the first time since Week 4, no NFL team is on bye. Nobody gets to blame the schedule this week. Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Bijan Robinson are back for Mailing It In. Kyren Williams and Harold Fannin Jr. are back for FROBOT. And those two teams play each other.

### MR. FROBOT (7-4) at Mailing It In (6-4-1)

FROBOT have won five straight and lead the league with 1,465.7 points. Mailing It In are the defending champions, they lead this series 7-6, and they won last year's meeting, also in Week 12. The computer likes FROBOT at 61%, and their best lineups project 135.1 to 122.0.

The stakes are mostly about the bye. A win puts FROBOT's bye odds at 69%. A loss drops them to 26%. Mailing are playing to stay in that race at all: 21% with a win, 1% with a loss. One thing to watch is Justin Herbert, Mailing's starting quarterback, who's questionable with a concussion.

## Around the league

**Bullish on da Bears (8-3) at Hauk Tua (6-5).** The 1 seed is a 40% underdog again. A win keeps Bullish's bye odds at 86%, and a loss cuts them to 46%. Brock Bowers (ankle) and Saquon Barkley (neck) are both questionable, and Brian D. still has to choose between Brock Purdy (225.3 points) and Caleb Williams (124.7) at quarterback. Hauk Tua have won the last two in this series, and they need it: 57% to make the playoffs with a win, 25% with a loss. Jahmyr Gibbs, the MVP leader, projects for 24.4. Bullish have been underdogs before. I'm only saying.

**Jesus Christ College (2-9) at Hold Timers (7-4).** No game moves more playoff odds this week. Hold Timers go to 88% with a win and 54% with a loss. They're 76% favorites against a team coming off a record-low 37.0. But JCC won this same Week 12 game last year, 99.9 to 85.1. This week, JCC start Anthony Richardson Sr. at quarterback. He projects for 16.1 and has 0.9 points this season.

**Son of Tinactin (3-7-1) at Fully Cooked (7-4).** This is the George Pickens game, in the same week as the trade. Pickens projects for 16.4 in his first game for Fully Cooked, against the team that sent him there. Fully Cooked are 83% favorites. A win puts their bye odds at 56%, and a loss drops them to 18%. Son of Tinactin lead the series 5-2, for what it's worth this week.

**Wholey Club (5-6) at Thots & Players (6-5).** Thots are 81% favorites. They go to 92% to make the playoffs with a win, or 73% with a loss.

**The Fist of the Magi (5-6) at Sour Grapes (3-8).** The Magi are 55% favorites and still technically alive. A win only gets them to 5% playoff odds. Sour Grapes have won the last two meetings, and Dak Prescott is now the All-FBP 1st Team quarterback.

One more date to circle. The trade deadline is Thursday, December 3, at 12 pm ET, a week from tomorrow. Fully Cooked already made their move. Jesus Christ College have Emeka Egbuka, DeVonta Smith and Jeremiyah Love on the trading block, if anybody else wants to make one.

Enjoy the turkey. I'll have the box scores Tuesday.
