Last August, this league bought seven quarterbacks at the auction for a combined $32. Patrick Mahomes went for $2. So did C.J. Stroud. The most anybody paid for a QB was $14, for Lamar Jackson. Kenneth Walker went for $66.

That market is about to disappear. In 2027 the lineup grows to nine starters, and the new one is a SuperFlex: QB, RB, WR or TE. Nobody is putting a running back there. Superflex turns this into a two-quarterback league, and quarterbacks score more than anybody else on the field.

We've been getting previews all season. Thirty-three times this year, a quarterback has scored 20 or more points on an FBP bench. The latest came in week 9, when Stroud put up 35.4 on Sour Grapes' bench. They started Dak Prescott, who scored 19.5. Under this year's rules there was nowhere to play him. Next year, that's a start.

## What changes

- **Lineup:** 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, **1 SuperFlex**, 1 DST. Rosters are 20 active, 3 IR and 5 taxi.
- **QB keeper limit:** a team can keep **two non-rookie QBs**, plus any rookie QBs from that year's draft. A QB who spent his rookie year on the taxi squad can be kept into year two, but he takes one of the two spots.
- **Rookie QBs get pricier:** rookie salaries now come from a formula based on where a player went in the NFL draft and where he goes in ours. The top price for a QB is **$12**, double the old top QB price of $6.

## The math

Twelve teams starting two QBs is 24 starters, out of 32 NFL starting jobs. Add byes and injuries, and the 24th QB is often a backup. The average FBP starting QB this season has scored 18.6 a week. Rank the rostered QBs by points per game and the 12th-best is right around that number, at 18.4. The 24th is down at 13.2. So the gap between a team with two real starters and a team scrambling for its second one is real, and it shows up every single week.

That's why the keeper limit matters as much as the slot. Without it, the teams already hoarding quarterbacks would just keep all of them and starve everybody else. With it, the surplus has to go somewhere: traded, or thrown back into the auction.

## Deadline day, Superflex edition

Today's NFL trade deadline gave us a small taste of how this works. Two FBP quarterbacks changed NFL teams in the last week, and both landed somewhere another FBP quarterback is hurt.

Hauk Tua's Kirk Cousins was traded to Minnesota today. The Vikings' starter, Kyler Murray, is week-to-week with a hamstring injury, and Murray belongs to Jesus Christ College. Last week, Mailing It In's Jacoby Brissett went to Baltimore, where he's expected to start Monday night with Lamar Jackson hurt again. Lamar belongs to the Fist of the Magi.

In a one-QB league, an injured starter's backup is a waiver-wire curiosity. In Superflex, he's a starter, and somebody else's bad news is your lineup.

## Best suited

**Son of Tinactin.** One of the worst records in the league, and still the best quarterback room in it. Love, Daniels and Young are all starters, and Mendoza is a first-round rookie on the taxi squad. They can only keep two, which makes the others the most valuable trade chips in the league this offseason. And they hold two 1sts, projected 1.02 and 1.09. Take the top rookie QB on the board and he doesn't count against the limit in his first year.

**Bullish on da Bears.** The 1 seed has Purdy at 21.4 a game and Caleb Williams at 18.4, both 27 or younger next fall, and the pair costs $25 combined. Bullish don't have to do anything clever. They just keep both.

**MR. FROBOT.** Burrow is putting up 24.9 a game, second only to Josh Allen, and Tyler Shough has given them 18.2 a game for $2. He costs $5 next year. That leaves Jalen Hurts as a surplus. He's on IR right now, but at $14 he's a starter some other team will pay for.

**Sour Grapes.** Stroud at $5 is one of the best value contracts at the position, and week 9 showed it. Prescott turns 34 next season, but at $15 he's a fine second starter for a year or two. They also hold the projected 1.03 and $210 to spend, tied for the most in the league.

## Worst prepared

**Mailing It In.** The defending champs are 5-3-1 and hold the 4 seed, and they've done it with the worst QB play in the league. Their starting QBs average 12.1 a week. They've gone shopping since: Drew Lock and Geno Smith are new. But Justin Herbert has given them 10.1 a game, Smith will be 36 at kickoff and costs $20 next year, and Brissett just took a job filling in for somebody else. Their 2027 1st belongs to Son of Tinactin. They need a starter, maybe two, and they'll be shopping in the most expensive QB market this league has ever had.

**Jesus Christ College.** Six quarterbacks, and the best of them is Malik Willis at 13.1 a game. Jaxson Dart is the long-term piece, but his season ended with a knee injury in week 2. Kyler Murray is hurt, and Minnesota just traded for Kirk Cousins. JCC hold the projected 1.05 and 1.12 and $205 to spend, so they're likely to come out of the rookie draft with a quarterback.

**Wholey Club.** Matthew Stafford is producing, but he'll be 39 at kickoff, and Daniel Jones has been a 9.1-a-game backup. The good news is they hold two 1sts, projected 1.01 and 1.11, and $210 to spend. A rookie QB at 1.01 is the obvious move.

**On the fence:** Thots have the league's best QB in Josh Allen, but he's $30 next year at 31, and the backups behind him, Cam Ward and J.J. McCarthy, have averaged 12.6 and 8.5. Fully Cooked lead the West with Mahomes at $5, but Sam Darnold is on IR and the rest of the room is Joe Milton and Jalen Milroe. Hauk Tua have Maye and Cousins scoring like starters, but Cousins turns 39 before next season and just changed teams. Fist of the Magi went from three starters for two spots to a $17 Lamar who has played five games, a Goff who scored 5.3 in week 9, and Michael Penix at 13.7. Hold Timers have Trevor Lawrence and a $12 Bo Nix who's put up 14.5 a game.

## What to do now

If you have three starting QBs, you can sell one now, until the FBP trade deadline at noon Eastern on Thursday, December 3, just before week 13. Or you can sell in the offseason, between the start of the new league year and keeper day. Wait past keeper day and the third one goes back into the auction for nothing.

If you have one, start shopping now. Every team's second quarterback just became a starter, and the price of the 24th-best QB is about to go up a lot more than the price of the 12th.

And if you hold an early 1st, this rookie class has quarterbacks the mocks like: Drew Mestemaker, Darian Mensah, CJ Carr and Arch Manning all go in the first round of the NFL mocks. A rookie QB doesn't count against the two-QB limit in his draft year. In year two, he does.
