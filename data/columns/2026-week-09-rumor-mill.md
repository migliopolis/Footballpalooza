You didn't hear it from me. But I've got people in the league office, and those people say there's been chatter about expanding the playoffs to seven teams in 2027. Nothing's on paper. Nothing's been decided. But they're talking.

## What's being floated

The qualifying rules would stay the same, just with one more seat at the table:

- **Seeds 1–3:** the three division winners
- **Seeds 4–6:** three wild cards on record, up from two
- **Seed 7:** the best all-play record among everybody left, the spot the 6 seed fills today

The big change is the bye. Today the top two seeds sit out the first round. Under the new setup only the 1 seed would. That's the NFL's conference bracket: 2 hosts 7, 3 hosts 6, 4 hosts 5, and the 1 seed waits. It's still three rounds, so the title game doesn't move.

## If it started this year

As of week 8, here's how it would shake out:

1. Bullish on da Bears (bye)
2. Mailing It In vs. 7. Hauk Tua
3. Hold Timers vs. 6. Thots & Players
4. Fully Cooked vs. 5. MR. FROBOT

Thots move from the all-play seed to the third wild card on record. Hauk Tua get in as the new 7 seed: they're tied with the Magi in all-play wins, and they've scored more points. The team that would feel it most is Mailing It In. Under today's rules the 2 seed is a week off. Under the new ones, it's a home game against a team that got in through the side door.

## Rex's take

More teams, more meaningful December games, more reasons for a 4-4 team to set its lineup. I like it. And make no mistake, this is the NFL's own bracket, which means the bye finally means something: earn the 1 seed or play the extra week.

If you've got an opinion, take it to the commissioner. I'm just telling you what I hear.
