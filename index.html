How Est. $ is calculated
Values are generated using real FBP historical auction data (2016–2022) combined with three factors:
1. Available rank — an exponential decay curve fitted to actual FBP results prices the Nth best available player at each position. The curve drops steeply because most of the money concentrates in the top few players.
2. Global rank quality — a player's actual overall ranking acts as a ceiling and quality check. A fringe RB24 as the best available RB won't fetch elite money; a true RB1 will. The blend shifts based on how elite the player actually is.
3. Rookie premium — rookies on long-term contracts command a bonus, especially elite RBs who contribute immediately.
Noise and occasional overpay are added to simulate real auction variance. Click Reroll for a different draw.
